# Deepcore — Cave Mining Company

A dependency-free single-page browser game built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. Progress saves automatically in the browser's local storage.

## How to play

- Mine glowing copper, quartz, and iron nodes, or press **Space** to swing.
- Watch your basket capacity and sell your haul from the Mine view.
- Spend cash in the Shop on five tiers each of pickaxes, storage, vehicles, and regen beacons.
- The Regen beacon now has two extra tiers: Quantum beacon and Genesis beacon.
- Unlock four caves with escalating fees: $2,000, $3,000, and $4,000. Travel between unlocked caves is free, and each cave has better minerals.
- Veins regenerate over time; buy a Regen beacon to shorten the cycle.
- Clicking a vein starts a timing mini-game. Better minerals require more targets and shorter reaction windows.
- Check the Field log for your expedition history.
